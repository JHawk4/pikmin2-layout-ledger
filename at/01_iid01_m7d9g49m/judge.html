Every layout was rebuilt from the seed read off the video and timed by the layout judge (CaveGen's All Treasures judge, with its walking, carrying, enemy and gate times weighted as the Pay Off Debt judge refit on real times, which matches All Treasures runs too). Layout luck is the judge's time against the median of 1000 random layouts of the same sublevel. Runner is what the runner did against the median run on this site, with the judge's difference between this layout and the median run's taken out, so a bad layout is not counted against the runner. Par is a median runner on a median layout: the diamonds below show the runner against par, luck included. Negative is faster, as on splits. 13 sublevels whose seed is uncertain are shown faded and never counted.
