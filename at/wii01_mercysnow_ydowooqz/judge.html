Every layout was rebuilt from the seed read off the video and timed by the layout judge (CaveGen's All Treasures judge, with its walking, carrying, enemy and gate times weighted as the Pay Off Debt judge refit on real times, which matches All Treasures runs too). Layout luck is the judge's time against the median of 1000 random layouts of the same sublevel. Runner is what the runner did against the median run on this site, with the judge's difference between this layout and the median run's taken out, so a bad layout is not counted against the runner. Par is a median runner on a median layout: the diamonds below show the runner against par, luck included. Negative is faster, as on splits. 27 sublevels whose seed is uncertain are shown faded and never counted.

Every sublevel

1000 random layouts (1st–99th percentile; wider = more common)this layout (green faster, red slower)the runner against par: a median runner on a median layout. The gap from tick to diamond is the runner’s own part

Through the run

Blue: the time the layouts handed the runner so far, against median layouts. Orange: the time the runner gained on the median run so far beyond what the layouts gave. Hover for the numbers; click to jump to the card.

The judge against the runner

Across: how much faster or slower the judge rates this layout than the median run's layout. Up: how much faster or slower the runner actually was than the median run. On the orange line the layout explains the whole difference; dots below it beat what the layout predicts, dots above it lost time beyond the layout.
