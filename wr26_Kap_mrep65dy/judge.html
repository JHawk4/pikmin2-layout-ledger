Layout luck is the judge's time against the median of 1000 random layouts of the same sublevel. Runner is what the runner did against the median run on this site, counting 2 pokos as 1 second, with the judge's difference between this layout and the median run's taken out, so a bad layout is not counted against the runner. Par is a median runner on a median layout: the diamonds below show the runner against par, luck included. Negative is faster, as on splits. 1 sublevel whose seed is uncertain is shown faded and never counted.
