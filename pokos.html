Pokos by sublevel

Pokos per sublevel against the median run (the median is under each sublevel's name). Green = more, red = fewer, – = not read, ? = an unusually large difference worth checking. Hover a cell for the treasures; click it to open the video.

How the pokos are counted

Each row is a run, each column a sublevel where at least one run differs from the median. 12 other sublevels, where every run got the median, are left out. Each treasure collected shows a panel with its value; the panels are counted in the video and matched to the treasures CaveGen places on that layout. Each panel also shows the run's running Total, so pokos from creatures are whatever the Total rose by beyond the treasure; they are shared among the cave sublevels between the two panels by time spent in each (never the surface, and treasures picked up on the surface count toward no sublevel).
