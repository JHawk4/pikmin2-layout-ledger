How to read a detection

Each sublevel's entry screen drops the cave name's letters with starting speeds drawn from the game's random number generator. The detector tracks every letter's fall (the boxes in the pictures: green = used in the fit, orange = matched but not used, for example while bouncing; red ticks = where the fitted fall puts the letter), fits one starting speed per letter, then finds the generator state that produces those speeds.

  • Letters: how many letters had a clean enough fall to measure. Each letter narrows the seed down about 50-fold, so 10 or more letters pin it down on their own.
  • Fit: the typical distance in pixels between a tracked letter and its fitted fall. Below about 0.2 px is a clean capture; above about 0.5 px something is off (a dropped frame, blur, a mistracked letter).
  • Score: how far the best seed's letter speeds are from the measured ones, in units of each letter's own measurement error, squared and summed over the letters (each letter's share capped, so one bad letter can't dominate) after removing a shared speed offset. Lower is better; about 1 per letter is a perfect match.
  • Next: the same score for the second-best seed. A big gap between the two means nothing else comes close.
  • Confidence: high needs 10+ letters, under 4 per letter and a gap of 30+; medium needs 7+, under 6 and a gap of 15+. A seed can also be confirmed by its neighbours: consecutive sublevels' seeds sit a few hundred thousand steps apart in the generator, which almost never happens by chance.

Letter seed is the random number generator's state when the title's letters start falling; the layout seed is its state when the floor was generated, a fixed number of steps earlier, and is what CaveGen needs.

Two pictures from the video show the floor as the sublevel starts: the comedown, the high, wide view behind the title while the pod comes down (often dim, so it is brightened), and the landing view, the first view once the pod has landed. The layout, drawn by CaveGen3, outlines both on the floor: the comedown in blue (the game holds this one still shot, nearly straight down, while the title is up) and the landing view in yellow, with an arrow for the way the pod faces, so the pictures and the layout should match.
